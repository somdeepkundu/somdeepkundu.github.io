About LitFest Companion

What's on now, where it is, and how to get there, for Sunday at Godrej Literature Live! The Mumbai LitFest 2026.

“Reading gives us hope.”

Anil Dharker (1946–2021), who started the festival
  • Drag the timeline to see what's happening at any hour. On the day it follows the clock.
  • Tap a venue on the map, or a session in the list, to see the route from your gate.
  • Bookmark sessions to build your plan. It stays on this device, flags clashes, and can remind you when it's time to walk over.
  • Tap the location button to see where you are on campus, or Set my spot if GPS is weak indoors.
  • Step-free shows the wheelchair-accessible entrances marked on the festival map and routes to them.
  • The festival asks booked guests to arrive 15 minutes early. Arrive-by times use that rule.

This is an unofficial companion, not affiliated with the festival organisers. The programme and map come from the printed festival guide. Check litlive.in for last-minute changes.

Developed by Somdeep Kundu, a PhD student of IIT Bombay working on Agriculture and Rural Water Management using Earth Observation and bottom-up ground data.
