        How the pins were placed. Every entry was searched on Google Maps and the web. Exact means the pujo's own map listing or a venue confirmed by 2024–26 coverage. Venue means the hall, ground or temple named in your list. Area means only the locality could be found, so the pin marks a station or neighbourhood. Repeated entries are merged and keep all their list numbers. Venues can move year to year; check with the organiser before a long trip.

        Dates follow the Bengal almanac (Kolkata) as published by Divine Hindu. Venue checks used Free Press Journal, Boldsky, LBB and Justdial. Distances are straight-line.
