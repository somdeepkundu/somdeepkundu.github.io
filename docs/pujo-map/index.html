    About this map

    How each pin was placed

    • Venue confirmed: the pujo's own Google Maps listing, or a venue named in 2024 to 2026 news coverage.
    • Venue from your list: the hall, ground or temple your list names, found on the map.
    • Approximate: the venue could not be found, so the pin marks the station or neighbourhood. These show as hollow pins.

    Entries that appear twice in the list are shown once, with both list numbers. Six pujos that turned up while searching are included and marked as not on your list. Venues can change between years, so check with the organiser before a long trip. Distances are in a straight line, measured from IIT Bombay until you turn on Near me or pick a spot.
