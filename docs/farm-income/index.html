    Before you quote these numbers

    1. These are receipts from crops alone, for six months. They are not household incomes: wages, livestock and other work are left out.
    2. Only paid-out costs are subtracted. Family labour, seed saved from the last harvest and the household's own land cost nothing here, so the real return to farming is lower.
    3. January to June is mostly the rabi season. States that rely on the kharif crop look poorer here than they would over a full year.
    4. Amounts are in 2013 rupees, not adjusted for inflation.
    5. The ranges cover sampling error only, not mistakes in recall or valuation. For small states the range is wide; quote it.
    6. Large farms pull the average up. The median household receives much less than the average, so quote both.

    Get these numbers yourself

    The same toolkit downloads any survey on microdata.gov.in, and in Google Colab it needs no coding.

    1. Make a free account on microdata.gov.in and create an API key on your profile page. 5 min
    2. Open the notebook in Google Colab and save the key in Colab's Secrets panel. 2 min
    3. Press Run all. The notebook downloads the files, checks them against NSSO's report and writes the state tables. 15 min
